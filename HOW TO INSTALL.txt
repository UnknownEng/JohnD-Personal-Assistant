JohnD by Maverick Technologies
==============================

INSTALL
1. Double-click JohnD_Setup_1.7.0.exe and follow the steps.
   Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because the app is new:
   click "More info", then "Run anyway".
2. JohnD starts when setup finishes, and its window opens.
   The first start downloads its speech files (about 190 MB). Keep the internet connected.
   You'll hear "John D online" when it's ready.

(The "JohnD" folder next to this file is the same program without an installer:
 copy the folder anywhere and run JohnD.exe. Give customers the Setup file.)

USE
- Say "John D", wait for the beep, then speak.   Or press Ctrl + Alt + J.
- You can also type in the JohnD window. The Help tab lists things to say.
- The round "J" icon near the clock: double-click to open the window,
  right-click for Settings, Pause microphone and Quit.

CONVERSATION AND SMART TASKS (optional, free)
In the JohnD window open Connections > Thinking brain and follow the two buttons.
Without it JohnD still does commands, weather, reminders, lookups and more.

LICENCE
JohnD needs a licence key. When it first opens it shows the Licence box:
paste the key you received and press Connect.

UNINSTALL
Windows Settings > Apps > JohnD > Uninstall.

REQUIREMENTS
Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), a microphone, 8 GB of RAM, about 2 GB of free disk space.
